# Steam Demo Release Plan

**Status:** Local integrated demo candidate; no public release or release date is authorized by this document. Windows-first free-demo distribution remains conditional on final-artifact checks, human evidence and any required platform approvals. The earlier sixteen-week schedule is historical planning.

## Current candidate scope

The candidate includes two alternative six-trial-plus-boss expeditions, eleven standard spell components, open/reflector terrain, optional four-slot runs, practice/comparisons/tutorial, recipes/profile recovery, forge checkpoints, workshop discoveries, manual sharing, factual debrief and keyboard/controller/accessibility paths. The Frost/Split/Repulse lab remains optional and unaccepted as standard balance. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), [roadmap queue](roadmap_queue.json), [SPELL_EXPERIMENTS.md](SPELL_EXPERIMENTS.md) and the [evidence ledger](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md).

Implementation, automated validation, human experience validation and release acceptance are separate states. The original task ZIP is unavailable; the user approved reconstruction, and detailed SF-008–SF-029 mapping remains unknown. Completing this reconstruction does not certify those original tasks.

## Candidate freeze and shipping checklist

Unchecked items are required evidence, not claims that the implementation is missing:

- [ ] Identify the frozen revision, source manifest, matching Godot/templates and exact downloadable artifact.
- [ ] Final-head import, all eighteen suites, bounded stress and CI pass; original defect scenarios rerun; no unresolved P0/P1.
- [ ] Forge and Archive complete victory/death/retry journeys pass for the named artifact; route/terrain/boss cues are readable.
- [ ] Recipes, backup/protected-file handling, no-save mode, sharing, four-slot limits and safe-forge resume work without duplicated rewards or inventory grants.
- [ ] Tutorial/practice and factual report are accurate; lab experiments remain explicitly isolated from normal rules.
- [ ] Keyboard-only and physical-controller paths, remapping, text entry, disconnect/reconnect, focus, scrolling and display-size readability are accepted on named devices.
- [ ] Representative graphical runtime and packaged performance meet declared target-hardware budgets; inspect workload counts, errors, p50/p95/p99/max and memory/object peaks.
- [ ] Downloaded Windows package passes headless smoke and human graphical launch/play/retry/settings/audio/scaling journeys on a fresh machine.
- [ ] Blind playtests record construction/comprehension, situational build usefulness, pacing and voluntary replay with participant counts and actual observations.
- [ ] Original and third-party license/provenance inventory matches the shipped art/audio/code; notices are bundled.
- [ ] Store text and captures represent the accepted candidate, identify experimental features accurately and make no unsupported pacing/device/performance claims.
- [ ] Feedback/support route, defect triage owner and rollback/hotfix plan are assigned.
- [ ] Required Steamworks account/store/build/demo-association reviews are complete, if Steam distribution is selected.
- [ ] The owner explicitly authorizes publication of the named artifact.

Use [TESTING.md](TESTING.md), [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md), [INPUT_JOURNEY.md](INPUT_JOURNEY.md) and [PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md) for procedures. An export or configured CI job alone does not close any human or platform gate. Historical evidence remains tied to its original revision.

## Platform operations

The owner must verify partner/account, tax/bank, payment, app/demo association, review and scheduling requirements directly in Steamworks before setting dates. This repository has not performed those account or publication actions. Consult official [getting started](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted), [Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon), [demo](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/application/demos) and [Steam Playtest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/playtest) documentation at decision time; no fixed current waiting period or approval guarantee is asserted here.

Invited direct builds or Steam Playtest can support validation before a public free demo. Any planned full-game store page should describe only truthful accepted/planned scope. Verify install, launch, updates and uninstall through the intended distribution path.

## Authorized launch and follow-up

After the explicit release decision, publish the accepted artifact, verify public visibility/install, monitor real reports and prioritize crashes, softlocks, data loss and progression defects. Record actual player observations and voluntary feedback; bounded local reports do not imply consent to an external analytics backend. Produce a dated continue/revise/stop decision using comprehension, replay reasons, production cost and remaining scope. Tests, wishlists and small samples do not prove commercial demand.
