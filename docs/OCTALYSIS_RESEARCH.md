# Octalysis Research and Roadmap Hypotheses

Access date: **2026-10-25**. Scope: the user-authorized reconstruction in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), based on `ad162de`. The original task ZIP is unavailable; this note does not recover its missing specifications. **No human evidence for these reconstructed features has been collected here.**

## Evidence and interpretation

Use Octalysis to generate questions about creative experimentation, mastery and ownership. The feature mappings and priorities below are **Spellforge design hypotheses**, not findings from the cited studies. No numerical Octalysis score, feature count or event total establishes fun, comprehension, balance or replay demand.

**Application:** offer meaningful choices, understandable consequences and usable controls, then observe participants. This is our inference from the [game self-determination research](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf), not a claim that Octalysis and self-determination theory are interchangeable. Treat the framework's left/right-brain labels as symbolic; Chou explicitly describes them that way in [Ownership & Possession](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-study/8-core-drives-4-ownership/).

## Eight drives applied to the approved direction

Drive names come from the [official overview](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-examples/octalysis-gamification-framework/). Everything in the feature/test columns is a proposed local design decision. **Core** supports the main loop; **support** follows the foundation; **optional** must earn its scope; **guardrail** limits pressure. Priorities are not numeric drive scores.

| Drive | Priority and concrete feature | User-observable acceptance question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Epic Meaning & Calling | **Support:** a brief expedition objective and workshop discovery framing give experiments a purpose within the existing fiction. | Can an unfamiliar player explain the next objective and why an experiment might help, without a lore explanation from the facilitator? Keep framing brief if it distracts from casting. |
| 2. Development & Accomplishment | **Core:** guided first experiment, legible enemy/boss cues and an actionable factual debrief support mastery; the optional four-slot challenge offers a visible constraint. | After failure, can a player identify an observed cause, propose one change and try it? Can they distinguish improving execution from obtaining a component? Challenge participation must be an informed choice. |
| 3. Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback | **Core:** ordered editing, honest current-versus-draft comparisons, deterministic practice and situational diagnostics close the experiment/feedback loop. | Before a test, ask the player to predict a changed slot's effect; afterward, can they explain what happened and choose a follow-up? Record mismatches. A preview must label its scenario and avoid claiming universal DPS or effectiveness. |
| 4. Ownership & Possession | **Core:** recoverable local profiles, named recipes and persistent discoveries/cosmetics preserve self-authored work. | Can a player name, save, relaunch and deliberately revisit their build? Do they describe a reason to keep or revise it? Restored/shared recipes must clearly distinguish the design from components owned in a run. |
| 5. Social Influence & Relatedness | **Optional:** explicit versioned recipe sharing allows an experiment to be exchanged without accounts or an online service. | Can another tester import a code, identify unavailable components and explain the shared idea? Ask whether exchange helped; do not infer relatedness from an export click. No popularity claims or social obligation. |
| 6. Scarcity & Impatience | **Guardrail:** the existing slot, mana, cooldown and reward-choice limits can create tactical trade-offs; an optional four-slot mode adds a chosen constraint. | Can players explain what they gave up and try another approach without waiting outside play? Check that rejected imports or capped builds give useful feedback. No energy timers, expiring progression or missed-day penalties. |
| 7. Unpredictability & Curiosity | **Support:** two alternative six-trial-plus-boss expeditions, seeded routes/terrain and a second boss invite different hypotheses. | Can players describe a tactical difference and choose a next experiment, rather than only notice different decoration? Verify threats remain readable and a seed is reproducible. Two alternatives do not become twelve consecutive trials. |
| 8. Loss & Avoidance | **Guardrail:** run stakes remain understandable; safe between-encounter checkpoints and persistent discoveries reduce accidental loss of time and authorship. | Can players explain what death loses and what survives? After quitting at a supported boundary, can they resume with the same reward eligibility and build? Observe whether failure prompts a voluntary retry or frustration. No permanent damage grind. |

The emphasis on drive 3 follows the author's [creativity/feedback account](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-study/8-core-drives-gamification-3-empowerment-creativity-feedback/); the prediction/explanation exercise is our test. The profile/recipe mapping similarly interprets [drive 4](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-study/8-core-drives-4-ownership/); persistence correctness alone cannot demonstrate ownership feelings.

## Lifecycle and review hypotheses

Use the author's [four-phase distinction](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-analysis/a-something-relationship/) as a review prompt: Discovery communicates the spell-building promise honestly; Onboarding enables one understandable experiment; Scaffolding supports repeated runs, saved recipes and resume; Endgame offers voluntary mastery, discoveries and the optional challenge. These are finite offline journeys, with no requirement to maximize daily retention.

For each PR, identify the affected hypothesis and observable disconfirmation:

- **Creative comprehension:** comparisons/practice help players predict consequences. Repeated unexplained surprises are evidence to revise feedback before adding content.
- **Mastery:** debriefs and cues produce a specific next action. Players saying only “I need more permanent damage” challenges the intended learning loop.
- **Ownership:** players deliberately revisit or revise a recipe after relaunch. Merely creating many saved entries does not establish value.
- **Replay choice:** players can articulate why another route/build is worth trying and may freely stop. Completion rate or extra minutes alone does not explain that choice.

These are review questions, not new numbered tasks or survey scales. Preserve eleven baseline components and six normal enemy archetypes as the starting point. Component cap 16 and enemy cap 9 are ceilings, not content quotas. Frost/Split ordering, a single-variable Split adjustment, Repulse and any additional enemy remain optional candidates requiring a rule contract, focused regressions and comparative observations. Do not accept balance on framework fit alone.

## Evidence and release gates

The [academic reflection](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3147/paper8.pdf) warns against apparent objectivity and adding disconnected techniques. Our consequence is to prefer a few coherent features, test the actual journeys and record disagreements, without treating a framework score as acceptance.

Use [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md) for directed and blind sessions. Record the build/seed/setup, participant's predictions and explanations, interventions, failures, and their stated stop/continue reasons. Separate observed behavior from interpretation; ask neutral questions. A bounded local report can corroborate recorded events but cannot infer unobserved damage, enjoyment or motivation.

Before accepting the reconstructed experience, collect human evidence for first-experiment comprehension, honest comparisons, practice, recipe recovery, each complete alternative expedition, death/retry, checkpoint resume, sharing failures, challenge constraints and complete supported controls/accessibility journeys. Re-test changed behavior at the final revision. Automated correctness/export checks, graphical fresh-Windows journeys, target-hardware performance, human usability and public-release authorization remain distinct gates; none is certified by this research note. Open details remain in [ROADMAP_GAPS.md](ROADMAP_GAPS.md).
